## Ideas Engineered Into Real-World Solutions.

### SIX SENSES

## Clinic Management System

**Industry:** Healthcare

**Category:** Custom Software Development

**Project Status:** Delivered

**Project Overview**

Six Senses is a specialized clinic management system developed to support the administrative, clinical, and financial operations of a healthcare facility.

The solution brings essential operational workflows into a unified digital environment, enabling more structured management of appointments, patient information, treatment sessions, and financial activities.

**Core Capabilities**
- Patient records and medical information management
- Appointment and treatment session scheduling
- Service and treatment package management
- Invoicing and payment tracking
- Financial and operational reporting
- Role-based administrative access
- Patient-facing digital services

**Engineering Approach**

The system incorporates structured backend architecture, centralized relational data management, API-based communication, and role-based access control.

**Project Value**

An integrated digital foundation designed to simplify healthcare operations and improve the organization of essential clinical and administrative processes.

**Live Website:** [sixsenses.clinic](https://sixsenses.clinic/)

### MEDICAL CLUB

## Academic Digital Platform

**Industry:** Medical Education

**Category:** Educational Technology Platform

**Client:** Medical Club — The Hashemite University

**Project Status:** Development & Deployment

**Project Overview**

The Medical Club Platform is a comprehensive academic digital ecosystem developed for the Medical Club at the Hashemite University in Jordan.

The project aims to transform the way medical students discover, organize, contribute to, and interact with academic resources.

Designed as more than a conventional educational website, the platform brings together learning materials, academic content management, examinations, research resources, and personalized educational workflows.

**Designed Capabilities**
- Academic learning resources
- Medical lectures and clinical materials
- Examination resources
- Research and educational publications
- Student contributions and content submissions
- Personalized learning dashboards
- Study roadmaps and progress tracking
- Administrative content management
- Academic search and resource discovery
- Role-based access and content governance

**Engineering Approach**

The platform's backend architecture incorporates structured academic data models, resource storage, authentication and authorization, administrative governance, search capabilities, and modular service design.

**Project Value**

A connected academic environment designed to improve accessibility, organization, and engagement with medical educational resources.

**Project Website:** [medclubhu.com](https://medclubhu.com/)
